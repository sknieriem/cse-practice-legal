Privacy Policy — CSE Practice

Effective Date: November 23, 2026  |  Developer: PJC Inc  |  App: CSE Practice (Android)

Short version: All 1,378 study questions work 100% offline, and we never run ads or third-party trackers. Your quiz history, study streaks, exam date, and optional inspiration photo stay strictly on your device. Google Play Billing processes your Reviewer Pass purchase history solely to verify and restore your pass.

1. Information We Collect

None directly. CSE Practice does not require an account and does not contain advertisements or third-party analytics trackers. All 1,378 study questions, hints, and Taglish explanations work 100% offline on your device. Internet access is used solely by Google Play Billing to verify or restore your Reviewer Pass purchase.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To help you study, the app saves the following information locally on your Android device using Android Room and DataStore:

  • Your practice answers, spaced-repetition review schedule, readiness estimate, XP, and daily streak.
  • Your optional target exam date, language preference (English or Taglish), and theme preference.
  • An optional personal inspiration photo if you choose to select one from your device.

This data never leaves your device unless you enable standard Android Auto Backup or manually share a progress/celebration image card using Android's share sheet.

3. Purchases & Google Play

Purchases of CSE Practice are processed exclusively by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment card or billing details. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how Google Play processes transactions.

4. Deleting Your Data

Because all study data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

CSE Practice is designed for adult and young-adult Civil Service Exam candidates and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

CSE Practice is an independent exam review application and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the Philippine Civil Service Commission (CSC) or any government agency. Official exam schedules, filing procedures, and guidelines are published by the Civil Service Commission at csc.gov.ph.

7. Changes & Contact

If this policy is updated in the future, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. For questions, privacy inquiries, or support, contact PJC Inc at csepractice.support@gmail.com (or via the developer contact listed on the Google Play store page).
